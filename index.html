📖 ASL Book Signer

Type words to see their ASL signs - Perfect for signing books!

How to use:

  • Fingerspelling mode: Shows individual letter signs for spelling words
  • Word signs mode: Shows common ASL word signs (when available)
  • Signed Exact English mode: Signs every word in English order, breaking words into root + affixes (e.g., "running" → "run" + "-ing")
  • Type any word or sentence and see the ASL signs appear below
  • Great for learning how to sign book content to your loved ones!
